The NCN SONATA 21 project **Dynamical Resource for Multipartite Quantum Systems (DyResQ)** will investigate dynamical multipartite entanglement, open quantum systems, algebraic invariants, and temporal quantum correlations at Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń.

## PhD position

A PhD position is planned for work on multipartite quantum resources, open-system dynamics, and quantum-information methods.

## Postdoctoral position

A postdoctoral position is planned for a researcher in theoretical quantum information, entanglement, or mathematical methods for quantum dynamics.

Formal calls will provide the definitive eligibility requirements, employment conditions, deadlines, and application procedures. Until those calls are published, prospective applicants may send a concise expression of interest to [giovanni.scala@poliba.it](mailto:giovanni.scala@poliba.it).
