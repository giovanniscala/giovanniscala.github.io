My NCN SONATA 21 project, *Dynamical Resources of Multipartite Quantum Systems* (DyResQ), asks how long entanglement survives when a quantum system interacts with its environment. We use theory and numerical simulations to study how noise affects entanglement shared across several particles and how measurements at different times can reveal it.

- **Postdoctoral researcher:** full-time, 210,000 PLN/year gross salary, initially 12 months, with the possibility of extension to 36 months in total. [University announcement](https://www.umk.pl/en/jobs/?task=offer&action=one&id=5014) · [EURAXESS](https://www.euraxess.pl/jobs/481916).
- **PhD research scholarship:** supplementary scholarship for students already enrolled in a doctoral school, PLN 3,000/month for 12 months. [Full announcement (PDF)](https://www.fizyka.umk.pl/panel/wp-content/uploads/PhD-Student-%E2%80%93-scholarship.pdf) · [NCN announcement](https://www2.ncn.gov.pl/baza-ofert/?akcja=wyswietl&id=234765) · [UMK faculty listing](https://www.fizyka.umk.pl/student/praca-dla-studentow-i-doktorantow/).

**Deadline:** 20 December 2026. **Starting date:** 1 February 2027.

Neither position requires a letter of recommendation. PhD scholarship applicants must have their supervisor’s agreement and satisfy Poland’s Higher Education and Science Act.
