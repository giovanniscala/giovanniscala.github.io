## Overview

There is an old, simple question that can help us understand a fundamental property of the universe. It is usually called Olbers' paradox, after the German astronomer Heinrich Wilhelm Olbers:

**Why is the night sky dark?**

This short outreach note is preserved in the site archive and is not promoted on the homepage.
