**Principal Investigator:** Giovanni Scala  
**Host:** Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń, Institute of Physics  
**Funding:** National Science Centre (NCN), SONATA 21, grant 2025/59/D/ST2/02708  
**Duration:** November 2026 – October 2029

DyResQ investigates how useful quantum correlations evolve in multipartite systems subject to noise. The project develops algebraic methods for quantifying and detecting entanglement, studies its lifetime in Markovian and non-Markovian models, and explores how temporal correlations can support dynamical witnesses. Analytical work is complemented by numerical simulations.

The long-term aim is to identify operationally accessible criteria for quantum resources in realistic dynamical settings, with connections to quantum communication, metrology and control.
